# Brainhole Contract Checks

Use these checks before accepting a generated idea:

1. Persona invariant: which known trait does the idea use?
2. Elastic zone: what can be exaggerated without breaking audience trust?
3. Forbidden move: what interpretation would make readers reject it?
4. Modern-system collision: what specific rule, metric, queue, form, KPI, contract, or platform creates the joke?
5. Serious underbelly: what real pressure sits below the comedy?
6. Protagonist stage: what can the protagonist legally do at this point in the growth curve?
7. Plot consequence: what changes after the joke lands?
8. Repair path: if the character appears to violate a core value, how does the story reveal cause, cost, and recovery?
